Speak Repeat: Free Speech Practice Game for Kids

Speak Repeat is a listen-and-repeat game for early speech practice. Children see He, She and They picture cards, hear the prompt, and repeat after it to practice pronouns. Made for preschool and pre-K children, ages 3 to 4, it is a free way to practice speech, pronouns and listening.
