Emergency Kids: Free Safety Practice Game

Emergency Kids teaches children what to do in an emergency through guided practice. Children practice dialing 9-1-1, match each helper, such as firefighters, police officers, medics and park rangers, to the emergency they help with, and learn safety basics like saying their home address out loud. Made for preschool and pre-K children, ages 3 to 4, it is a free way to practice calling 9-1-1 and learn who helps in an emergency.
