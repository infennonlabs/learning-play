Calendar Kids: Free Days, Months & Seasons Game

Calendar Kids teaches the three core time topics for early learners. In Days of the Week, children learn weekday order and names. In Months of the Year, they practice all 12 months in order. In Seasons, they match each season to its weather. Made for preschool and pre-K children, ages 3 to 4, it is a free way to learn the days of the week, the months of the year and the seasons.
