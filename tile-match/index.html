Tile Match Learning: Free Pattern Matching Game

Tile Match Learning builds pattern recognition with a tile-matching game. Children match pairs of free tiles on the board, or switch to equation mode, where they select two number tiles and solve the equation. Hints remove a wrong answer or select the right one, and a new board starts when no matches are left. Made for children from preschool through 5th grade, ages 3 to 11, it is a free way to practice pattern recognition and early math with a tile matching game.

Tile Match Learning is part of InfennonLabs, free early learning games for children from PreK through 5th grade, with no subscription and no distracting characters.
