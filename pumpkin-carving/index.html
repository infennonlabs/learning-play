Pumpkin Carving: Free Halloween Game for Kids

Pumpkin Carving lets children carve a pumpkin with their finger, then light it up for the night. They pick a pumpkin, draw around a shape and let go to close the cut, use Slice to cut lines, and tap or drag to punch round holes. They can start the pumpkin over, or choose Carve it for me. Made for children ages 3 to 12, it is a free way to carve a virtual pumpkin for Halloween.

Pumpkin Carving is part of InfennonLabs, free early learning games for children from PreK through 5th grade, with no subscription and no distracting characters.
