Comic Kids: Free Comic Book Maker for Kids

Comic Kids lets children make their own comic book. They pick a page layout, choose a scene for each panel, and add heroes, villains, robots, pets and props, then change each character's face to happy, sad, surprised or angry. They write the story in speech bubbles, thought bubbles, shouts, captions and sound effects, add more pages, read the finished comic back, and save up to five comics to come back to. Made for children ages 3 to 12, it is a free way to practice storytelling, writing and creativity.
