Does It Solve: Free Math Logic Game for Kids

Does It Solve builds early math reasoning in six modes. More or Less asks whether answers are more or less than a target, with an Advanced version that compares each step. Equation Builder builds equations that equal a target number, Math Solver solves each equation, and Equation Match All selects every expression with the same value. Random Timed Session runs a timed round of a length the child chooses.

Does It Solve is part of InfennonLabs, free early learning games for children from PreK through 5th grade, with no subscription and no distracting characters.
