Countin Kids: Free Counting Game for Preschool

Countin Kids builds counting skills in six activities. Count the Number picks a target and counts from 1 step by step, Count Down counts back to 1, and Count By skip counts by 2s, 5s, 10s and more. Flashcard Count shows emoji groups on the front and the number and number word on the back. Find Missing Number fills gaps in counting sequences, and Match Items to Number asks children to choose every item that shows the target number. Made for preschool, pre-K and kindergarten children, ages 3 to 6, it is a free way to practice counting, counting down, skip counting and number recognition.
