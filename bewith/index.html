Be With God: Free Catholic Bible & Prayer Game for Kids

Be With God helps children grow in their Catholic faith through four activities. In Bible they flip through illustrated stories read aloud, starting with the creation and the first people in Genesis, and tap any word to hear it. In Prayer they listen to the Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be and Grace Before Meals, then fill in the line and put the words in order. Biblical Word Search finds words from the stories, and Our Faith explains the Trinity, Mary, the Mass and more. Made for children from preschool through 5th grade, ages 3 to 11, it is free and plays in your browser.
