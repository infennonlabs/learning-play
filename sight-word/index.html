Sight Word Search: Free Word Search & Spelling Game

Sight Word Search practices sight words and spelling in five activities. Children search for sight words in a word search, fill in missing letters, choose the correct spelling, listen and spell, and complete sentences such as "I see ___ dog in the park" with the right sight word.

Sight Word Search is part of InfennonLabs, free early learning games for children from PreK through 5th grade, with no subscription and no distracting characters.
