Paint by Number: Free Coloring Game for Kids

Paint by Number lets children color a picture two ways. They can match the numbers to the colors, or use any colors they like by picking a color and tapping a space to paint it. Children can move between pictures and clear their painting to start again. Made for children ages 3 to 12, it is a free way to color pictures by number or with any colors they like.
