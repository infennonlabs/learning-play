Earth Lab: Free Earth Science Game for Kids

Earth Lab teaches Earth science. Children drill down through the crust, mantle and core, push plates together to build mountains and volcanoes, follow a drop of water around the water cycle, dive a submarine through the ocean zones, climb a rocket through the layers of the sky, split light into a rainbow, and use a compass and magnets to see how Earth's magnetic field shields us from the Sun. It has seven topics and 24 activities that read the questions aloud, with hints that cross out a wrong answer or place a card. Made for 3rd, 4th and 5th grade children, ages 8 to 11, it is a free way to learn the layers of the Earth, plate tectonics, mountains, water, the atmosphere, light and magnetism in the browser, with no subscription.
