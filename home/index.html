InfennonLabs: free early learning games for kids

InfennonLabs makes free, simple, focused learning games for children from preschool and PreK through 5th grade, ages 3 to 12. There is no subscription and no distracting characters, just clear practice with the alphabet, counting, math, reading, science, money, time and everyday life skills. The games were built from experience with the author's own two kids, and they play right in your web browser.

  • Alpha Kids – Emphasis on the alphabet and associated words and sounds
  • Calendar Kids – Learn days, months, and seasons through guided play
  • Emergency Kids – Learn what to do in an emergency through guided practice
  • Does It Solve – Simple math logical thinking
  • Tile Match Learning – Fun pattern recognition learning
  • Make It – Critical thinking and spelling through recipe creations
  • Math Adventures Kids – Build strong math skills through patterns and problem solving
  • Memory Match – Card-flip memory games designed for recognition and recall
  • Reading Writing Kids – Read and write with confidence
  • Sight Word Search – Word search and spelling practice
  • Slice It – Slice, balance and compare fractions with fruit, scales and pizza
  • Chem Kids – Chemistry exploration and periodic table learning
  • Countin Kids – Learn counting through playful number activities
  • Speak Repeat – Pronoun and speech practice through listen-and-repeat play
  • Grammar Adventures Kids – Grammar and sentence skills through playful challenges
  • Word Blend Kids – Build and match word joiners
  • Realm Breaker – Break bricks, follow a quest, and read picture books
  • Pumpkin Carving – Carve a pumpkin by finger, then light it up for the night
  • Piano Practice – Play piano by ear with songs, chords, and matching music symbols
  • Cooking Kids – Cook up reading, spelling, and math with ingredients and recipes
  • Paint by Number – Color a picture by number, or paint it any way you like
  • Anatomy Kids – Peel back the body from skin to skeleton and learn about every part
  • Farm Kids – Run a farm: spell seeds, learn the seasons and count your coins
  • Comic Kids – Create your own comics. EXCELSIOR!
  • Arcade Lab – Arcade games for everyone
  • Make Change – Learn coins and bills, roll values, and how to make change
  • Clockwork – Learn the parts of a clock and practice telling time
