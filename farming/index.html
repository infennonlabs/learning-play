Farm Kids: Free Farming Game with Spelling & Math

Farm Kids lets children run a farm while they practice spelling, the seasons and counting coins. They send the tractor to clear wild land and the horse and plow to turn bare earth into a field, buy seeds at the Seed Shop, plant, water and harvest crops, and sell them to earn coins to buy more land. They also spell seeds letter by letter, and in winter everything rests until spring.

Farm Kids is part of InfennonLabs, free early learning games for children from PreK through 5th grade, with no subscription and no distracting characters.
