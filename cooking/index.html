Cooking Kids: Free Reading, Spelling & Math Game

Cooking Kids builds reading, spelling and math skills with ingredients and recipes, guided by a chef bear. Children hold and drag recipe steps into the right order, spell each ingredient, find ingredient words in a pantry word search, and count and measure ingredients in Measure & Scale. They can also drop ingredients in and see what they cook.

Cooking Kids is part of InfennonLabs, free early learning games for children from PreK through 5th grade, with no subscription and no distracting characters.
