Math Adventures: Free Math Skills Game for Kids

Math Adventures Kids builds math skills through patterns and problem solving in ten modes. Children practice addition, subtraction and multiplication, solve longer equations in guided steps, find missing numbers in counting sequences, solve number ladders, and solve for x in simple equations. They also find emoji groups that match a target number, connect numbers to matching groups, and solve short story problems with emoji clues and spoken prompts. Made for kindergarten through 3rd grade children, ages 5 to 9, it is a free way to practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, number patterns and word problems.
