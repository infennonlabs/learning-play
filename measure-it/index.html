Measure Market: Free Measurement Game for Kids

Measure Market teaches US customary measurement. Children scoop and level flour with measuring cups, trade teaspoons for tablespoons, fill a jug in cups, pints and quarts, weigh things on a balance scale in ounces and pounds, stretch a ruler along an object in inches, feet and yards, scale a recipe up or down, and pick the sensible unit in an estimating game. Hints cross out a wrong answer or do one step for the child. Made for 3rd, 4th and 5th grade children, ages 8 to 11, it is a free way to practice units of measurement, fractions and unit conversion.
