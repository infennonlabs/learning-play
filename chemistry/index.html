Chem Kids: Free Chemistry & Periodic Table Game

Chem Kids is a chemistry exploration game built around the periodic table. In Explore the Table, children learn about each element, including its category, year discovered, electron configuration and common compounds. In Build Chemicals they combine elements, such as hydrogen and oxygen to make water, and in Lab Experiments they watch the reaction and run it again. Made for 5th grade students, ages 10 to 11, it is a free way to explore chemistry and the periodic table.
