Hidden Treasures: Free Seek-and-Find Game for Kids

Hidden Treasures is a seek-and-find game. Children search ten scenes, from a toy room and a backyard garden to a jungle, a coral reef and a castle attic, for the objects on their list. Each item shows a picture and a word, so even children who cannot read yet can match by sight. Hints and warm-or-cold clues help when a child is stuck, and the game gets easier or harder on its own as they play. Made for children from preschool through 5th grade, ages 3 to 11.
