Realm Breaker: Free Brick Breaker and Reading Game for Kids

Realm Breaker is a fantasy game with three modes. In Brick Breaker, children bounce a magic orb to break bricks across 20 levels, beat bosses and collect power-ups. In Realm Quest, they read a branching story, pick a hero and a friend, and choose what happens next. In Story Books, they read illustrated chapter books, with 5 books of 4 chapters each, at their own reading level. Every story can be read aloud, and tapping a word says it. Made for 1st, 2nd and 3rd grade children, ages 6 to 9, it is a free way to practice reading.
