Piano Practice: Free Online Piano Game for Kids

Piano Practice teaches children to play piano by ear with songs, chords and matching music symbols. In Play Along they press the highlighted keys to play songs such as Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Row Row Row Your Boat and Mary Had a Little Lamb. In Play This Chord they tap the keys that make a chord, and in Connection Mode they drag each music term to its matching symbol. Made for children ages 3 to 12, it is a free way to practice piano, chords, songs and music symbols.
